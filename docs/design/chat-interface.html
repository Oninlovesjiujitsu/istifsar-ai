Can you tell me about the authorship of Al-Ibānah and its significance in Islamic theology?
history_edu Archivist Inquiry Response

The document is titled "The Creed of Abul-Hasan Al-Ash’ari: Al-Ibānah ‘an Usūl Ad-Diyānah" [1].

It was written by al-Imām Abul-Ḥasan al-Ash’arī (d. 324 AH), a central figure whose transition back to the methodology of the Salaf is documented extensively within this specific text. The work serves as a formal refutation of Mu'tazilite doctrine and an affirmation of traditionalist creed [Source 2].
